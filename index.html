Virtual Orbit Readiness (VOR)

Quiet Assistance in the Classroom.
Real-Time Focus & Sensory De-Escalation.

Vori is not a gaming distraction. It is a wearable assistive technology designed for school class time that senses early indicators of sensory overload and attention drift, presenting subtle visual cues to prevent meltdowns and maintain classroom engagement.

In-Situ Visual Prompts

Calming peripheral micro-visuals triggered automatically prior to behavioral escalation.

Zero Classroom Disruption

Keeps neurodivergent students focused without drawing peer attention or stigma.

Preemptive Neuro-Feedback

Real-time biometrics & eye-tracking to catch overreactivity before outbursts occur.

Vori Technology

Sensory-Friendly Software + Hardware Integration

Vori Neuro-Feedback Software

Algorithmic vision and eye-tracking module that senses behavioral micro-changes (fixation drop, erratic movements) and deploys unobtrusive sensory grounding protocols in real-time.

Ultra-Lightweight Form Factor (<90g)

Overcomes the #1 barrier identified in parent discovery: sensory aversion to heavy VR headsets or rigid glasses. Built using medical-grade soft-touch materials weighing under 90g, with non-wearable 3D projection fallback.
